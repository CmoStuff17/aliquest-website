Aliquest Education at AXL Academy

Hi families! I'm Ami, and I'm excited to help lead Aliquest's program at AXL Academy. After years working with children and families, I've seen how much of a difference it makes when a child has someone who believes in them, meets them where they are and makes learning feel manageable.

We care about academics, but we also care about the whole child. Students should have a place where they can slow down, ask questions, practice skills, build confidence and be proud of their progress.
